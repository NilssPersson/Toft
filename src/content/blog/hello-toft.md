Toft is a cosy farming game about a small island of your own.

Plant a crop, water it, and harvest it when it is ready. Every harvest fills a slot on the wheel. Spin with one slot filled and you have a one-in-eight chance, or wait until all six are full and the multiplier comes into play. Win enough spins and you level up, unlocking new crops along the way.

The prototype is playable now. Head to [Play](/play) and give it a try. Your island is saved in your browser.

Later on, you will be able to visit friends' islands and watch them farm live.
